A series of tools I built to improve my QoL, purposefully kept free, without ads and open to the public.

## Live sites

### 🚛 [Brisbane Loading Zone Map](https://loadingzone.xyz)
Find a commercial-vehicle loading zone near you in Brisbane, on your phone. Search any place or address, showing nearby zones with their parking restrictions, days/times, and live status, with one-tap directions. Tracks your location live as you drive.

### 🎬 [myhoyts](https://myhoyts.xyz)
Find a HOYTS cinema session today where nobody has booked a seat yet. Pick your state and a HOYTS cinema, and it lists today's sessions with zero seats booked. Click a time to jump straight into the Hoyts booking.
